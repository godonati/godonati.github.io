02 · Work case study

Globo video autonomy

Helping a high-volume branded-content workflow produce more independently, with less repetitive work and more room for creative decisions.

The context

Make a high-volume workflow feel possible.

At Globo, I oversaw the video-autonomy segment for branded soccer commercials. The work sat at the intersection of creative production, technical systems, and the practical need to deliver a large number of independent files every week.

03

Use a repeatable system to generate branded videos without rebuilding every variation by hand.

04

Keep people in control

Reduce repetitive workload while preserving the creative judgment that makes the work good.

The product lesson

Good automation removes friction, not ownership.

This experience sharpened how I think about product work: the best systems do not simply make teams faster. They make the right work easier to see, make quality easier to maintain, and give specialists more time for the decisions only they can make.
