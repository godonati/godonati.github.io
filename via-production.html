01 · Product case study

Via Production

A scalable video production management platform for marketing teams that need to move faster without losing the human part of the craft.

The opportunity

Make quality content
easier to scale.

Marketing teams had strong ideas and plenty of content demand, but not a reliable way to coordinate editors, prioritize work, and understand production capacity. Via was designed as the operating layer between the brief and the finished video.

10×targeted output increase
20%planned cost reduction
3 daystarget lead time
90%satisfaction goal

Product thinking

One platform,
four connected moves.

01

Brief

Turn a marketing need into a clear, actionable production request.

02

Match

Assign work to the right editor with visibility into capacity and fit.

03

Create

Give editors structure, context, and room to make great creative decisions.

04

Learn

Track throughput, cost, and satisfaction to improve the system over time.

Roadmap

Start focused.
Grow with evidence.

The MVP focuses on the workflow foundations: task management, editor assignment, production monitoring, and reporting. Expansion follows validated usage—not a wish list.

Phase 01

Foundation

Architecture, Kanban workflow, assignment, and editor management.

Phase 02

Expansion

Real-time monitoring, creative freedom features, and beta testing.

Phase 03

Optimization

Cost tracking, performance tuning, and a full launch informed by feedback.
